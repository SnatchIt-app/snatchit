### Seed Round // Miami // 2026

**One-liner:** The marketplace for nightlife access.

---

## GLOBAL DESIGN SYSTEM (apply to every slide)

**Palette**
- Background: `#000000` (true black, matte)
- Primary text: `#FFFFFF`
- Accent: `#FF0033` (Miami blood red — used on 1 hero element per slide, never more)
- Subtext / metadata: `#6B6B6B` (cold gray)

**Typography**
- Display / headlines: **Helvetica Neue Condensed Bold**, all caps, tracking `-2%`, line-height `0.95`
- Body: **Helvetica Neue Regular** or **Medium**, sentence case, tracking `0%`, line-height `1.4`
- Body in caps for callouts only (stats, phase labels). Never paragraph.

**Layout rules**
- 16:9. Always.
- 8-column grid. Hero text anchors to column 1 or column 5, never center.
- Massive whitespace — 40% of every slide is empty. Whitespace is the flex.
- One red accent element per slide. One. Not two.
- No shadows. No gradients. No rounded corners. No glow except where specified (Cover, Solution, Ask).

**Aesthetic**
- Brutalist editorial meets luxury nightlife.
- Think: Supreme lookbook × Ferrari keynote × Basquiat catalogue × a Berghain flyer.
- Feels like Miami at 2AM — neon bleeding through smoke, velvet against concrete.
- Every slide should look like it could be a poster on a loft wall.

**Motion (for the live deck)**
- No slide transitions. Hard cuts only.
- One element appears with a 50ms red flash, then settles white. That's the only animation.

## SLIDE 1 — COVER

### Exact Copy
```
SNATCH IT

THE MARKETPLACE FOR
NIGHTLIFE ACCESS.

Miami  //  Seed round  //  2026
```

### Strategic Intent
Plant the category, not the product. "Marketplace for nightlife access" positions SNATCH IT as a category-defining company before the investor has read a single other word. No tagline about bidding. No app talk. This is a brand announcing itself.

### Visual Design Direction
- **Layout:** Full-bleed black. Logo wordmark **SNATCH IT** at optical center-left in 180pt red (`#FF0033`). Tagline in 84pt white condensed bold, stacked in two lines directly below. Metadata bottom-left corner, 14pt cold gray.
- **Image:** No photo. Subtle red radial gradient bleeds from the bottom edge — 5% opacity max. Reads like neon halation from a street-level sign seen from a window.
- **Feel:** A poster. A flyer you'd keep. Not a corporate title page.

# EXECUTION NOTES

**Build order:** Lock copy. Grayscale the layouts. Add red last. Kill anything that fights for attention with the one red element per slide.

**Photography:** For Slide 4, shoot original. Do not use stock. A single cinematic Miami-nightlife photo is worth $20K of deck credibility. Shoot at LIV or E11EVEN with a 35mm, ISO pushed, 1/60 sec, tungsten-heavy WB.

**Deck variants:**
- **Sent deck:** 14 slides, static.
- **Live deck:** 12 slides — cut Why Now and Competition (you'll answer those in Q&A), lean harder on Cover → Problem → Solution → Traction → Ask.
- **Teaser / one-pager:** A single slide version is just Slide 2 + Slide 4 combined. Use it for cold outreach.

**Opening line for the live pitch:**
> *"Last Saturday, a guy at Liv paid $4,200 on Instagram for a table the venue would've sold for $1,500. That's the problem. We're going to fix it."*
