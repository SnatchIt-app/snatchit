## 1. GAP ANALYSIS — THE 7 THINGS BLOCKING A $3M RAISE

1. **No Team slide.** At seed, the check is on the founder. No founder = no check.
2. **Supply is a black box.** "37 venues signed" is unverifiable. Needs deal terms, exclusivity, take rate, length.
3. **No unit economics.** Marketplace investors underwrite economics, not vision. Take rate alone ≠ UE.
4. **Traction is vanity metrics.** Waitlists and pledges don't count. Real GMV and repeat rate do.
5. **TAM is PowerPoint math.** $85B is fabricated. Needs bottom-up, cited, defensible.
6. **Competition is wrong.** Ticketmaster/Resy/StubHub aren't the real competitors. Dorsia, promoter WhatsApp, and concierge services are.
7. **No risk acknowledgment.** Silence on risks reads as naiveté. Name the killers and show the mitigations.

## 5. INVESTOR THINKING — WHAT MAKES THIS A "MUST INVEST"

#### The single sentence that closes the round
> *"Every scarce experience on earth is priced wrong. SNATCH IT is the infrastructure that fixes it — and they already own Miami."*

That's the line an investor pitches to their partners. If the deck delivers that sentence clearly, the check follows.

#### The single metric that 10x's investor conviction
**46% lift per auction, sustained across 420+ completed transactions.**

Not the waitlist. Not the venue count. Not the press. The *lift number.*

Because 46% lift means:
- Venues make more → supply side compounds forever
- Bidders keep bidding → demand side is addicted to the urgency
- Take rate expands → 15% is floor, not ceiling
- The marketplace has genuine price discovery → defensible pricing data moat

If SNATCH IT has this metric on 400+ real transactions, the round is oversubscribed in 3 weeks.

If they don't — they should spend the next 60 days running that exact experiment before raising.

#### Second-order signals that turn this into a "fight for allocation" round
1. **A household-name nightlife operator co-signs as advisor** (e.g., David Grutman, Michael Capponi)
2. **Lead investor is a marketplace-native VC** (Benchmark, A16Z Consumer, Sequoia Surge) — not a generalist
3. **One viral moment** — a real auction on Instagram, $5K+ bid on TikTok, one celebrity using it publicly
4. **A second city opening** — even a pre-signup waitlist in Vegas or NYC with named venues attached

Any one of these four lights the round on fire.
