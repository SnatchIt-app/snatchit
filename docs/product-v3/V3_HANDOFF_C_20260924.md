# B → C — the approved footer (owner 2026-09-25), and one regression to avoid

The owner approved **Option B** in both appearances, superseding the 2026-09-24 Buy-Now-first
hierarchy. Bid leads, filled and red, its minimum inside the pill; Buy Now second, outlined, with
its quantity-aware label, price and sub-line exactly as rendered. Only in `auction_and_buy_now` —
no other state inherits this pair.

## Do NOT implement it by swapping `primary` and `secondary` in the resolver

I checked the wiring at `91062ab1`. A swap in `listingActions()` looks like a one-line change and
ships a real regression.

**1. Buy Now would silently lose its busy state.** `ListingDetailScreen.tsx:1444` binds
`pendingLabel="Reserving…"` and `loading={primaryBusy}` to the **primary slot, positionally**. The
secondary `BarAction` at `:1455` has **neither prop**. Buy Now is the only action that sets
`reserving`. So after a swap, tapping Buy Now starts the reserve call and the control **looks
idle** — CFT-203's "visible while the reserve call is in flight" dropped without a test noticing,
because no test asserts the secondary slot's busy state.

**2. The dead wiring moves the other way.** `primaryBusy` (`:1273`) is keyed on **kind**, not
position, so it correctly evaluates false for `place_bid` — which means "Reserving…" would sit
mounted and never-shown under "Place a bid". Harmless on native (`accessibilityElementsHidden`)
but it is wiring that no longer means anything.

**3. `primary` would quietly change meaning.** `tests/listing-detail-state.test.ts:134,156` and
`tests/v3-harness-variants.test.ts:205` assert `primary.kind === 'buy_now'` for this mode. Those
encode **eligibility** — the resolver's chosen action. Flipping them to satisfy a paint ruling
merges "the action the resolver chose" with "the loudest button", which is the exact conflation
the owner's "preserve action eligibility and behavior" is guarding against.

## Do it as presentation, separate from eligibility

Keep `primary` / `secondary` meaning what they mean. Add explicit presentation to the resolved
pair — an order and a fill per action, decided in one place and tested there — then have the
footer render the pair in that order with that fill.

Then make the busy wiring follow the **action kind rather than the slot**, which is the fix that
makes the ruling safe in either direction:

* `loading` for an action ⇐ `(kind === 'buy_now' || kind === 'continue_reservation') && reserving`
* `pendingLabel="Reserving…"` on those same kinds, wherever they are drawn

With that, order and fill become free to change and nothing behavioural moves with them.

## What I will verify on the committed result

Both appearances at the identified commit; that no other state inherits the pair (sold, cancelled,
won, reserved-by-you, on-hold, own-listing, not-found all unchanged); that Buy Now still shows its
busy state on press; that the bid's minimum stays **inside** the pill and Buy Now keeps its
quantity-aware label, price and sub-line; and measured truncation at 393 / 375 / 320 pt plus the
narrow-width proxy. **Dynamic Type, wrapping and safe areas are native-class and are checked on
the simulator, not on the web path.**
