09 / 17
How the venue
side works.
Planned model · pilots in motion
Inventory source
Last-minute release of tickets, VIP, and access that would otherwise be sold through promoters or left unsold on the night.
Venue value prop
Price discovery. Higher yield on the inventory they'd discount anyway. Real demand data instead of guessing.
Venue protections
Reserve price. Inventory caps. Ability to pause or pull at any time.
Commercial terms (target)
Non-exclusive pilots first. Weekly settlement. 10% total take on cleared bids (5% / 5%).
First wave
Miami nightlife operators — small set of pilot venues. Founder in active conversations.
Why venues say yes
Promoters keep 30–50% of a sale. We charge 5% — and we bring demand data, a younger audience, and real-time pricing. Lower take. Better signal.
