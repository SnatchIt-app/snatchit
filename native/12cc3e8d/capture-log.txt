TWO TRAPS FOUND IN THIS WINDOW, BOTH OF WHICH VOIDED FRAMES BEFORE THEY WERE CAUGHT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. `xcrun simctl ui <dev> appearance` DOES NOT DRIVE THIS APP. app/_dev/v3-profile.tsx sets the
   theme from the app's own stored preference via useAppearancePreference, so the route needs
   ?appearance=light|dark explicitly. My first A3XL frame came back DARK while simctl reported
   appearance=light, because an earlier session had left the stored preference on dark. Frame
   discarded and recaptured. This is the same contamination that nearly produced a false
   "system appearance does not work" finding in an earlier batch.

2. `content_size` CAN SILENTLY REVERT ACROSS THE RELAUNCH. Setting extra-extra-extra-large reads
   back correctly, and then after the dev-client relaunch reads back as
   accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large. Observed on 3 of 5 attempts. shot.sh reads the size back
   only AFTER the capture, so the first pair of frames I took were labelled 3XL while the device
   had reverted to A3XL. The capture is now gated on three readbacks — after setting, after the
   relaunch, and after the capture — and any disagreement voids the frame without writing it.
   The two mislabelled frames were deleted.

   A CORRECTION TO MY OWN WORKING: I first claimed those two frames were "byte-identical" to the
   A3XL pair. They were not. I had compared a float32 sum of a cropped band, which saturates and
   collides, instead of the md5 I had used correctly on C's nine frames an hour earlier. The
   frames were distinct; the reason to discard them was the failed size readback alone. Identity
   claims in this log are md5.

   The safest reading of both traps: READ THE SIZE AND THE APPEARANCE OFF THE PAINTED FRAME, and
   treat simctl's readback as corroboration, not as the record.
