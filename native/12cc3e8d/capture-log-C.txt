THE TEXT SIZE ON THIS MACHINE CANNOT BE TRUSTED FROM simctl ALONE
------------------------------------------------------------------
Something on this machine resets the simulator's content size roughly every 30 seconds. It is
not my scripts: set / terminate / openurl all preserve A3XL, and the size also dropped during 30
seconds of pure idle with nothing launching. I could not identify the process.

Two consequences, both of which produced wrong-looking evidence before they were understood:
  * The app reads the content size AT LAUNCH. Setting A3XL under a running app leaves the paint
    unchanged — verified: both size reads said A3XL while the painted glyphs measured at the
    default scale. A frame is only A3XL if the size was A3XL when the app STARTED.
  * shot.sh sleeps 42 s during a relaunch, which loses the race. tools/v3-capture/fastshot.sh
    polls instead and completes in ~15 s, inside the reset window. Every frame above was taken
    with it, with the size read immediately before and after the capture; a frame whose two
    reads disagree is void and was discarded.

The decisive check is not simctl's answer but the PAINT: a known-A3XL frame fingerprints at
145-158 px tall ink bands, the default at 60-92. Each frame above was checked against that
before being kept. Several earlier attempts in this window were discarded on exactly that test.
