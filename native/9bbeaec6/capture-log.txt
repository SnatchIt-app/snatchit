METHOD NOTES, kept because they cost time
-----------------------------------------
1. The first capture attempt failed with "app never came up". That was not the method: it was the
   cold-start bundle (2357 modules, first launch after a fresh Metro) outrunning cap.sh's 90 s
   banner gate. The splash screenshots and Metro's own "iOS Bundled 10559ms" line confirm it.
   Warm relaunches afterwards took well under the gate.
2. Two frames came back as the Sign in screen. The first was the auth gate (snatchit://settings);
   the second was a dropped deep link on an otherwise identical command. BOTH were deleted, not
   filed. A frame that reaches the wrong screen still passes the banner gate and still passes the
   size bracket — the gate proves the app is up, not that it is where you asked it to go. The
   band fingerprint caught the second one before I looked at it, because the measured structure
   did not match the retained frame's.
3. The pixel diff is what finally distinguishes "right screen" from "plausible screen". Zero
   differing pixels below the status bar against a known-good frame is a stronger identity check
   than reading the screenshot.
