GESTURE METHOD — and what it says about the Home "scrolling defect"
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Three distinct causes of a "drag did nothing", each identified by a control, none of them a
product defect:
  1. The drag landed on the FIXED FOOTER. At A3XL the Listing footer occupies roughly the
     bottom 300 pt, so a drag starting at y=700 pt is on the sticky bar. Moving the start to
     the content band (y 460 → 170) scrolled immediately.
  2. Two touch paths injected back-to-back: the second is dropped. The identical path sent
     again after a ~20 s gap scrolled. Every gesture here is therefore preceded by a >= 2 s
     pause, and each scroll is verified by diffing the frame before overwriting anything.
  3. A fast release flicks with momentum and overshoots. A damped tail (three samples with
     <= 2 pt of movement and 60/120/150 ms gaps) lands where it is aimed.

HOME FEATURE: a drag that starts INSIDE the feature card scrolled on 3 of 4 trials and opened
the listing on 1. The amount is therefore REACHABLE — it is captured above, at A3XL, not at a
smaller size — so there is no product scrolling defect to report. The one navigation is the
press winning a marginal press/pan decision under synthetic injection; a real finger was not
available to me, so I state the trial counts rather than a verdict on the gesture itself.
Nothing in the app's interaction was changed for any of this.
