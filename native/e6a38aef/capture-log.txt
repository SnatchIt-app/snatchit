label for a small one-line label on EVERY row, to fix one word.

So this is left as it is, deliberately, and it is an owner/B call rather than mine: the text is
complete and identifiable, and the remaining fault is one mid-word break on one label. If that
is judged unacceptable, the options are the one-line shrink above, or shorter copy.
