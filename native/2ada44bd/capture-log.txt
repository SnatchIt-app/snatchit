WHY THE RATE IS BACK HERE AND STILL GONE AT CHECKOUT
-----------------------------------------------------
Checked in the code, not taken from the relay: this row's amount is
listingPriceLinesV3().fee -> buyerFeeCents(cents) -> round(cents × BUYER_FEE_RATE), and
BUYER_FEE_RATE is 0.10. The number is computed by the app, in this file, at exactly ten percent,
so the label describes arithmetic that just happened. At CHECKOUT the amount comes from the
order's own breakdown and the venue primary rail carries its own buyer_fee, so a rate there
would be a promise the app cannot keep. Both labels are right for different reasons — which is
why one rule could not cover both, and why W-2 was over-broad.

The label is RESALE_FEE_LABEL, built from BUYER_FEE_RATE the way PlaceBidScreen already builds
"Fee (10%)". Restoring a typed "(10%)" would have left two literals free to drift.

A TOOLING TRAP THAT ALMOST PUT A WRONG FRAME IN THIS FOLDER
------------------------------------------------------------
I served with `git merge --ff-only … | tail -1 && … && exec npx expo start`. A pipeline exits
with the status of its LAST command, so `tail` returned 0 when the merge had refused, the `&&`
chain carried on, and Metro came up on the commit WITHOUT the restore. The first frame I took
was of the old code and was discarded. What caught it was reading HEAD rather than the command's
output. Never pipe a command whose exit status you are about to depend on.
