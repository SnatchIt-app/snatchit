TWO METHOD NOTES, BOTH LEARNED BY LOSING FRAMES
-----------------------------------------------
1. `simctl io screenshot` CANNOT WRITE TO THE EXTERNAL VOLUME. The screenshot is taken by the
   CoreSimulator service, not by the shell, and that service is refused:
     NSCocoaErrorDomain code=513 … "You don't have permission to save the file" (EPERM)
   This shell writes to the same directory without trouble. The first attempt of this window
   lost every frame to it SILENTLY, because cap.sh sent simctl's stderr to /dev/null. cap.sh now
   stages the capture on the internal disk, copies it into place, and fails loudly instead.
2. `touch_path` was dropped repeatedly on this screen while `swipe` moved it the same distance
   immediately. Every scroll here is a `swipe`, and each one was confirmed by comparing frames
   before anything was kept. A dropped synthetic gesture is not a product scrolling defect —
   the screen scrolls, as these frames show.
